# TikTok research, wave 3 (2026-11-02)

A research workflow, three lenses then a skeptical critique of the 10-day plan: the SoberTok niche (live TikTok page data), app slideshow playbooks, and how TikTok treats new accounts and scheduled posts. The schedule in `tiktok/queue.py` follows the critique.

## Critique and plan changes

**(1) Eight changes, ranked by expected impact**

1. **Lead with video.** At least 4 of every 5 posts should be video. Make the default a 5 to 12 second text loop over original motion: the tree swaying or glowing, the timelapse, or a recording of the app's screen. Cap carousels at 1 in 5, use them for save-worthy lists, and judge them on saves rather than views. The plan currently runs carousels in 3 looks as the main track. [Metricool 2026: video gets 5x the reach of image posts. About 50 top #sobertok and #sobercurious posts were all video. @reframeapp and @werestillsober grow on 5 to 12 second loops.]

2. **Rebuild the hook list around the hooks proven in this niche.**
   - Second-person POV posts tied to high-risk moments. Put "POV: it's Friday night, you didn't drink" in Fri Nov 6's evening slot and "POV: it's Saturday morning, you stayed sober" in Sat Nov 7's morning slot. Skip weekday versions; the Monday one got 8K views.
   - One "Ever notice...?" body-signal hook a day: hangxiety, the 3 a.m. wake-up, sleep score, puffy face.
   - A weekly builder "ask the room" question, such as "What did you think was normal until you stopped?"
   - Move identity hooks ("day 1 vs day 100") and A-or-B polls to the test pool.
   [@werestillsober POV data; Reframe hooks; @itscourtneycain got 914K views at 808 followers; question posts get +26% comments.]

3. **Retime the slots** to the times in section 2. Drop 12:45, which no evidence supports. [PMC9246905: craving peaks around 21:00 local time. Metricool: the platform peaks from 6 to 9 p.m.]

4. **Split the CTA into soft and hard.**
   - Soft (POV, questions, signs lists, breathing): the app appears only in the caption or pinned comment, and the post ends on a question.
   - Hard (the timelapse with the app card, day counter, builder posts): the full "Search Sober Sobriety Day Counter" line.
   - Alternate short captions (one keyword line plus a question) with long builder-story captions, and log which CTA each post used.
   [Quickthrottle on soft vs hard CTA; Larry's CTA rotation; caption-length sources conflict.]

5. **Fix sound on photo posts.** "Default sound" is untested.
   - On the first Studio Photos upload, confirm you can attach a sound from TikTok's Commercial Music Library (CML) and that the post fires as a photo post.
   - If that fails, save carousels as drafts and add the sound by hand in the app. Never ship a silent post.
   - Upload the original score once as the account's own original sound and reuse it.
   [Silent slideshows lose reach (Larry, Reelflood). Metricool says Studio's scheduler handles videos only. "Your brand" disclosure limits sounds to the CML.]

6. **Turn "Your brand" disclosure on for every post, warm-up included.** The handle is the brand and the tree is the app's own art, so "value-only" posts can still read as promotion. TikTok says disclosure does not change recommendations, while undisclosed promotion can lose For You eligibility. [Disclosure findings: the requirement is high confidence, no reach effect is medium.]

7. **Freeze most variables for this run.** At a minimum of 5 posts per test group, days 3 to 10 (24 posts) support about 4 groups. Test only video vs carousel, and one hook type against another within video. Freeze look, slide count, app-slide position and CTA wording until day 11.
   - Make the native look the default: white bold text with a black outline, or TikTok-style white boxes.
   - Keep the cream-serif night look off slides 1 to 3, since designed graphics underperform.
   - Rules for slide 1: 10 words or fewer, a number or a concrete moment, no app.
   - Keep all text between y=192 and y=1536 (out of the top 10% and bottom 20%).
   [About 20 posts per format test (Reelflood); native-text finding; hook rules; Larry's text spec.]

8. **Retarget hashtags.** Use 3 to 5 tags: one broad (#sobertok or #alcoholfree), one intent (#quitdrinking or #sobercurious), and 1 or 2 topic tags matched to the hook (#hangxiety, #greyareadrinking, #mocktails). Drop #soberjourney (no data) and #sober (about 8.6K views per post vs 16 to 33K for the topic tags). [Hashtag views-per-post data.]

**(2) Best 3 posting times (PT)**

| Slot | Gap from blocked slot | What it hits |
|---|---|---|
| 07:15 | 45 min after 06:30 | 10:15 AM ET. Use it for "you made it" morning posts. |
| 16:15 | 75 min before 17:30 | 7:15 PM ET, the start of the ET craving window and of the 6 to 9 p.m. platform peak. |
| 18:30 | 60 min after 17:30 | 9:30 PM ET, 8:30 PM CT, 6:30 PM PT. This covers the 21:00 craving peak for most US viewers. Put the craving-moment posts and Friday's POV here. |

Optional: on Sat and Sun, move the morning post to 05:30 PT (8:30 AM ET, 60 min clear of 06:30). That matches when the Saturday-morning hits went up (about 5:20 to 7:00 AM PST). After 30 posts, check the Followers-activity tab in Studio and adjust.

**(3) Formats for days 1 and 2 (no app, disclosure still on)**

- 7-second POV text-over-tree videos with no end card. The tree must move.
- Box-breathing guide videos, e.g. "8 p.m. craving? breathe with this for 60 seconds."
- 5-second question loops, e.g. "What did you think was normal until you stopped?"
- 1 or 2 "Ever notice...?" body-signal videos, with sourced wording only.
- At most one carousel in the morning look (a signs list) to set a baseline for saves.
- Keep the timelapse with the app card and the day-counter posts for day 3 onward.

**(4) Suppression risks in the plan**

- **No disclosure on days 1 and 2** while the brand handle and the app's tree art appear in the posts. Undisclosed commercial content can lose For You eligibility, and repeat misses can bring posting limits.
- **Photo posts on default or silent sound.** Studio photo scheduling may also fail or post as private. After each of the first 3 photo posts fires, check that it is public, has views and shows as a photo post.
- **"Minimally edited" loops.** A 7-second POV over a static tree image can read as low-effort and lose For You eligibility. Build loops from original footage with real motion.
- **Near-duplicates.** Don't post the same content in 3 looks, or the same carousel with a new slide 1, as separate posts. TikTok does not recommend duplicated content.
- **Health claims** in "sober math" and signs lists. Keep money and time math. Phrase body changes as "many people notice" with a named source (NIAAA, CDC). Never say treat, cure, detox or diagnose, and never give withdrawal advice. Signs lists must not read as a diagnosis, which is also an App Review 1.4.1 risk.
- **Music.** Use only the original score or CML sounds, never label-owned trending songs, on any post with "Your brand" on.
- **Visuals.** No drinks, bottles, pouring or bars. Mocktails, empty glasses, morning light and the app's screens are fine.
- **AI images.** If any image is AI-generated, turn on TikTok's AI-generated label.
- **Automation.** Don't script likes, follows or comments. Studio uploads should go one at a time with human-like pacing and no fast retries.
- **0-view guard.** If 3 posts in a row sit at 0 to 20 views after 24 hours, pause the schedule. At 24 hours, also check Studio analytics for an "ineligible for recommendation" flag and appeal right away.

## Findings

- **Scheduling in TikTok Studio web does not by itself reduce reach. The claim that scheduled posts get suppressed is folklore: nothing from TikTok supports it. The real cause of zero-view posts is posts going out as private, not a ranking penalty.** (medium (no reach penalty: inferred from official ranking docs plus a vendor test; SELF_ONLY rule: official))  
  Evidence: TikTok's own explainer of how it recommends videos lists viewer-side signals such as completion, shares and rewatches. It says follower count and previous high-performing videos are not direct factors, and it never mentions how a video was uploaded. The only test of scheduled vs native posting is Metricool's, and Metricool sells a scheduler. TikTok's developer docs say content posted by unaudited third-party API clients is restricted to SELF_ONLY (private), which explains most 'scheduled posts got 0 views' reports. Studio is TikTok's own tool, so that limit does not apply to it. One conflict: Metricool says Studio's native scheduler handles videos only, not carousels, while the kit schedules from the Studio Photos tab.  
  Source: https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you ; https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines ; https://metricool.com/does-scheduling-social-media-posts-affect-reach/ ; https://www.zoomsphere.com/blog/does-scheduling-kill-your-tiktok-reach-in-2026 ; https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/introducing-video-scheduler-now-you-can-plan-tiktoks-in-advance  
  Action: Keep scheduling in Studio web. For the first 3 scheduled photo posts, open the profile after each one fires and confirm it is public, has views, and shows as a photo post. Add a visibility column to Tracker.csv. If scheduled photo posts come out private or broken, switch carousels to app drafts and keep Studio scheduling for videos only.
- **3 posts a day on a new account has no documented penalty. Data shows more posts raise the top end of views, not the typical post. Two things are folklore: that posting a lot puts you in '0-view jail', and that TikTok officially recommends 1 to 4 posts a day (that figure has no primary source).** (medium (large observational dataset run by a vendor; no TikTok statement on daily caps))  
  Evidence: Buffer studied 11.4M posts from 150k+ accounts, comparing each account against itself over time. Against 1 post a week, views per post rose about 17% at 2-5 posts a week, 29% at 6-10 and 34% at 11+, with no drop at high volume. Median views stayed flat (about 460-510) while the 90th percentile went from 3.7k to 14.4k. The gains held across account sizes. The study does not test daily cadence on its own. Separately, TikTok says it does not recommend duplicated content and generally avoids showing two videos in a row from the same creator or with the same sound.  
  Source: https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/ ; https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you  
  Action: Keep 3 a day, as long as every post is a distinct idea. Never post near-duplicates (the same carousel with a new hook) as separate posts. Expect most posts to land near the median. Keep the plan's 72h per-type median test, and judge types by their best post as well as their median.
- **TikTok says the disclosure label does not change recommendations, while leaving commercial content undisclosed can cost For You eligibility. For a brand account this is official policy. That it applies to 'Your brand' specifically is an inference.** (high for disclosure being required and undisclosed posts losing eligibility; medium for 'Your brand' having no reach effect)  
  Evidence: TikTok's help page says that turning on the disclosure setting and labelling a post as a Paid partnership 'will not affect the way TikTok recommends content'. It also reports an internal study with 'no performance difference', and says undisclosed commercial content 'may not be eligible' for the For You feed. The Community Guidelines require disclosure when you promote your own business or product. Undisclosed posts can lose visibility, and repeat failures can bring posting restrictions or a ban. Developer docs show that choosing 'Your brand' attaches a Music Usage Confirmation, and 'Branded content' adds the Branded Content Policy. Vendors (not TikTok) say 'Your brand' shows a 'Promotional content' label. TikTok's no-impact statement names Paid partnership, not Your brand.

- **A Business account only sees Commercial Music Library (CML) sounds. That limit is official. Two claims are folklore: that a Personal account gets more reach, and that switching to Personal is a safe way to use trending sounds on promotional posts. TikTok publishes nothing on photo-post sound rules specifically.** (high for the CML limit; low for any reach difference between account types (no evidence either way))  
  Evidence: TikTok Help: on a Business Account, Add Sound shows only CML sounds, because the licenses for music outside the CML do not cover commercial use.
