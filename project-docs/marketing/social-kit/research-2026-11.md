# TikTok growth research, November 2026

Prepared 2026-11-02 for the @soberdaycounter experiment. Sources are cited inline. Many are vendor blogs, so each claim is tagged: **[data]** from a named dataset or study, **[self]** self-reported by a seller or founder, **[infer]** my inference. Nothing here was verified against TikTok's own live policy pages (the official URLs returned 404 to the fetcher), so check the policy items in section 3 by hand before launch.

## 1. What performs in sober content on TikTok

### Scale and tone

- Hashtag totals reported in 2023: #sober 4.6B views, #sobriety 2.2B ([Ypulse](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2023/01/13/young-people-are-turning-to-tiktok-to-help-them-quit-drinking/)). #alcoholfree was reported at 1.5B ([Ria Health](https://riahealth.com/blog/tiktok-culture-alcohol/)). Dated figures, no 2026 hashtag total was findable. **[data, old]**
- A University of Edinburgh study (28 interviews, 800 videos, FYP sampling Oct 2023 to Jan 2024) finds counting sober days is a core trope that creates accountability, and that tension exists between "sober curious" and "sober serious" creators ([AoIR paper](https://spir.aoir.org/ojs/index.php/spir/article/view/14025), [Edinburgh record](https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/first-glass-of-wine-in-8-months-an-examination-of-sober-curious-c/)). **[data]**
- Creators reach the sober-curious through "day in my life" and storytelling formats ([Ypulse](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2023/01/13/young-people-are-turning-to-tiktok-to-help-them-quit-drinking/)).
- Good Beer Hunting's read of SoberTok: confessional openers, recovery-journey series, coaching/education, accountability lectures. One example hit 2.8M views, 110K likes, 13K comments. Views collapse when a creator relapses (one post at 476 views). Moderation talk draws harsh comments, and one moderation account shut down ([Good Beer Hunting](https://goodbeerhunting.com/let-go-or-get-dragged/2023/1/3/were-scrolling-together)). **[infer]** A neutral, builder voice with no streak to lose avoids that fragility.
- Single viral examples: Chip Hafford's day-3 "pouring out beer cans" video passed 6M views ([WAGM](https://www.wagmtv.com/2022/07/30/chip-hafford-goes-viral-tik-tok-his-sharing-journey-sobriety/)). Chuck Norflis' early-recovery dance sparked a hashtag with 72.5M views ([Sober Curator](https://thesobercurator.com/sober-spotlight-a-casual-conversation-with-chuck-norflis-known-by-over-67k-followers-on-tiktok-as-soberlutionchuck-on-getting-sober-staying-sober-and-the-first-year-of-sobriety/)). Both depend on a personal story, which we cannot use.

## 2. Faceless / app-account formats and playbooks

### Published slideshow playbooks

| Playbook | Numbers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Oliver Henry "Larry" (Snugly app) | 500K+ views in ~5 days, top post 234K, 4 posts over 100K, $588 MRR after week 1, ~$0.50 per slideshow, ~60 sec human time ([Postiz](https://postiz.com/blog/ai-agent-tiktok-views-postiz-api), [stack-junkie](https://www.stack-junkie.com/blog/how-to-automate-tiktok-marketing-with-openclaw-larry-skill-deep-dive)) **[self]** | 6 slides, hook text on slide 1 (6.5% font), CTA slide 5, follow slide 6. Posted as drafts, trending sound added by hand. Limit 5 hashtags, story-style caption. |
| Stronger Mobile (gym app) | 725 videos in 295 days, 11 over 1M, 24 over 350K (about 50x median), 85 over 65K (about 10x median), 98% of top posts were slideshows ([Shortimize](https://shortimize.com/blog/the-slideshow-strategy-that-generated-700000-users-in-275-days)) **[self, vendor]** | Slide 1 is a selfie plus short text hook, release slide shows the app by slide 2 or 3. Hooks are short open loops, often mildly controversial inside the niche. Comments asking "what app" help conversion. |
| Astro app | 385 slideshows in 105 days on one account (about 3.7/day), median 37K views, 12 over 1M ([Shortimize](https://shortimize.com/blog/tiktok-multi-account-slideshow-strategy-making-50m-views-per-month)) **[self, vendor]** | Identity content (signs, compatibility), reveal structure, self-identification drives shares. Language-specific sub-accounts. |
| Couple Joy / Starla's (relationship apps) | 2M+ views / 65M+ views, $300K MRR claimed ([Playkit](https://playkit.substack.com/p/couple-joy-faceless-slideshow-growth)) **[self, paywalled]** | Final slide tells people to search the app name in the App Store instead of using a link. Slow consumption lets couples discuss each slide. |

Patterns across playbooks:
- Volume is the common factor: 2 to 5 posts per day, then keep what wins and clone it. Median post is far below the top post (50x), so expect most posts to do little. **[infer]**
- Larry's best hooks put another person plus a small conflict up front, and self-focused feature hooks scored 879 to 2,671 views vs 100K+ for story hooks ([Postiz](https://postiz.com/blog/ai-agent-tiktok-views-postiz-api)). **[self]** Our adaptation: hooks about the viewer's situation, not app features.
- Larry's diagnostic loop: high views and low installs means fix the CTA slide, low views and high installs means fix the hook, low and low means fix both ([stack-junkie](https://www.stack-junkie.com/blog/how-to-automate-tiktok-marketing-with-openclaw-larry-skill-deep-dive)). We will use the same loop.
- Warm-up claim: 7 to 14 days of normal use before automated posting. Vendor guidance is conflicting and unverified ([Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/tiktok-account-warm-up/)). Cheap to follow, so scroll and like in the niche 15 minutes a day for the first week. **[infer]**
- Skeptic note: faceless app accounts fail when nothing legible happens in the first seconds ([search summary](https://thesolocreators.com/learn/faceless-tiktok-slideshows-free-traffic/)). Our slide 1 must work alone.

### Photo carousel vs video (conflicting evidence)

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| Fanpage Karma 2025 | Carousel reach about 3% above video, engagement rate 81% higher ([Fanpage Karma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/carousel-vs-video-performance-tiktok-instagram/)) **[data]** |
| Stack Influence | TikTok told creators carousels get 2.9x comments and 1.9x likes. Shares about 33% fewer per post ([Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/tiktoks-photo-post-comeback)) |
| Buffer, March 2026, 45M posts | TikTok video median engagement 3.39%, 77% above carousels and photos ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/data-best-content-format-social-media/)) **[data]** |
| Metricool early 2026, 2.3M posts | Video got about 5x the views and 6 to 8x the interactions of image/carousel posts ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-study/)) **[data]** |

Reading: the biggest 2026 datasets favor video on raw views, partly because far more video is posted and because photo accounts skew to small creators. Older and TikTok-sourced claims favor carousels on comments, saves and engagement. No source gave carousel save rates. **Decision:** keep carousels as the base (42 built, zero marginal cost) but run 3 to 4 video arms per week so format is a tested variable, not an assumption.

### Formats worth testing for a faceless dev account

- Slideshow carousels (built): hook, 2 tips, app, close.
- Text-on-screen video over bonsai footage or app screen recording.
- Screen recording of the craving mode breathing exercise with a one-line caption (calm, ASMR-adjacent, loopable).
- Bonsai growth timelapse: "day 1 to day 100" tree growing in the app (the app's unique visual).
- POV text posts ("POV: it is day 12 and the tree just grew a branch").
- Comment-reply videos using TikTok's native reply-with-video ([TikTok newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/product-tutorial-reply-to-comments-with-video)), answering real comments with screen recordings or a text card.
- Polls and quizzes via question cards and "comment A or B".
- Builder-voice posts: "I built X because Y" and changelog posts. No findable published data on these in this niche. **[gap]**

## 3. Policy and platform constraints

### Alcohol and regulated goods

- TikTok's Community Guidelines (effective Sept 13, 2025) merged alcohol, tobacco, drugs, gambling and weapons into one "regulated goods and services" policy that bars trade, marketing and promotion of regulated, prohibited or high-risk goods ([ConductAtlas summary](https://conductatlas.com/platform/tiktok/tiktok-community-guidelines/regulated-goods-and-services-restriction/), [TikTok newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/community-guidelines-update-newsroom)). Secondary source: alcohol ads, alcohol clubs and alcohol-sponsored events are not allowed ([trade summary](https://www.meininger.de/en/wine/news/wine-ads-now-permitted-tiktok-usa-and-canada)). Note that source title implies wine ads were later permitted in some cases for registered businesses, so the policy has moved. **[verify live]**
- Ad policy excludes content that glamorizes or gratuitously depicts drinking ([TikTok ad policy handbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/ad-policy-handbook-north-america?lang=en)). Content promoting drinking by minors is banned.
- I found **no source saying recovery or sobriety content is itself restricted**. A 2022 study found large organic recovery communities on TikTok ([PsyPost](https://www.psypost.org/2022/04/study-investigates-how-people-are-using-tiktok-as-a-part-of-their-substance-use-disorder-recovery-63002)). **[infer]** Staying out of trouble means: no bottles, glasses, pouring shots or bar scenes, no brand names, no "drink alternatives" that mimic alcohol branding.
- Mental health rules ban content that promotes self-harm, and a separate misinformation rule covers false claims that could cause significant harm (Community Guidelines Sept 2025, per the [Social Media Today summary](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-updates-community-guidelines-to-provide-more-protection-for-users/618510)). Searches for self-harm terms surface support resources. **Safe practice:** keep every health claim inside the app's NIAAA-sourced catalog with "many people" wording. Keep the withdrawal safety line (do not stop suddenly after heavy daily drinking without medical advice) on every post that touches day 1 to 7 effects. Heavy withdrawal can be dangerous ([Surely summary](https://www.drinksurely.com/a/blog/what-happens-when-you-stop-drinking)).

### Branded content

- A business promoting its own product uses "Your brand" in Disclose post content, which adds a "Promotional content" label ([TikTok help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-the-content-disclosure-setting-for-creators)). The kit already says to enable it on every post. Vendors report TikTok may notify within hours if undisclosed branded content is detected, with a 24 hour fix window ([Skeepers](https://community.skeepers.io/blog/tiktok-new-policy/)). One vendor claims scheduled posts are auto-marked "Branded Organic" from Sept 2025. Unverified.

### Scheduling, timing, tags

- TikTok Studio web schedules 15 minutes to 10 days ahead, cannot edit a scheduled post (delete and re-upload), and TikTok's announcement covers video, so **photo-post scheduling is unconfirmed** ([Sked Social](https://skedsocial.com/blog/schedule-tiktok-photo-carousels.md), [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-adds-post-scheduling-to-tiktok-studio/736952)). A "30 day" window appears on blogs but has no TikTok source. Check the Photos tab shows a schedule toggle before filling a week.
- Photo post API limits: JPEG or WebP, up to 35 images, 20 MB each, title 90 chars, description 4,000 chars ([Sked Social](https://skedsocial.com/blog/schedule-tiktok-photo-carousels.md)). Inbox-mode posts cannot attach music automatically.
- Best times are contested. Sprout (2B engagements, Nov 2025 to Feb 2026): Tue to Thu 2 to 6 PM local, Sunday weakest. Buffer (7.1M posts): Saturday best, Sunday 9 AM the top slot, evening 6 to 11 PM strong ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok), [Sprout via IQFluence](https://iqfluence.io/public/blog/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok)). Both measure engagement, not reach. Our 6:30 AM, 11:30 AM and 5:30 PM PT slots straddle both. **[infer]** Treat slot as a variable: rotate which format sits in which slot so slot and format are not confounded.
- Hashtags: sources agree on 3 to 5 targeted tags, with caption keywords mattering more than tags, keyword in the first 80 to 125 characters ([Sprout](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/tiktok-hashtags/), [Social Kit](https://socialk.it/en/blog/tiktok-caption-writing-guide)). Vendor claim that slide text is indexed for search is unverified. Practice: put the search phrase (e.g. "alcohol free tips", "dry january") in slide 1 text and the first caption line, plus 3 tags.
- New-account behavior: TikTok tests each video on a small audience, and a "newborn shadowban" is mostly a testing phase, with no verified safe post count ([AI Video Cut](https://www.aivideocut.com/blog/tiktok-shadow-ban-algorithm)). Three posts a day is within what Larry and Astro ran. Do not repost watermarked or duplicated carousels.

## 5. Seasonal calendar, Nov 2026 to Jan 2027

Data points:
- 48% of US adults did not plan to consume alcohol or cannabis over the 2024 holidays, up from 42% in 2022. 42% of drinkers call themselves sober curious, up from 33% ([CivicScience](https://civicscience.com/americans-plan-for-a-more-sober-holiday-season/)). **[data]**
- 77% of drinkers aged 21 to 44 expect to drink the same or more over the 2025 holidays ([Talker Research](https://talkerresearch.com/how-young-americans-are-balancing-drinking-during-the-holidays/)). So an audience is under pressure in the same weeks.
- Dry January: 19% of US adults took part in 2026, up one point. The share who did not finish nearly doubled from 12% to 20%, and 49% of Gen Z adults aged 21 to 29 who tried did not finish ([CivicScience](https://civicscience.com/dry-january-on-the-rocks-participation-is-up-but-completion-rates-slip/)). 2025 participation was 22% in a different poll ([Morning Consult](https://pro.morningconsult.com/analysis/dry-january-participation-mainstreams-in-2025)). Different pollsters, so do not compare.
- UK: 17.5M adults planned a January break. Top reasons: saving money 21%, health 20%, weight 15% ([Alcohol Change UK](https://alcoholchange.org.uk/blog/uk-brings-a-jan-do-attitude-to-better-health-and-wellbeing-in-2026)). Money and calories are exactly our "sober math" format.
- Search interest in Dry January concentrates around Dec 30 to Jan 5 per one analysis ([Pantry & Larder](https://pantryandlarder.com/?p=3035519)) and has risen every year since 2014 per Google's trends editor ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/02/24/dry-january-february-athletic-beer)). Alcohol-free search overall peaks in April, not January ([Measure Protocol](https://www.measureprotocol.com/insights/dry-january-consumer-what-happens-in-february)). **[data, mixed]**

**Sequencing to peak into Dry January:**
1. Nov 3 to 22: find the two or three post types that beat the account median. Everything after reuses them.
2. Late Nov to mid Dec: build the audience with holiday-situation content (people looking for scripts search it now and follow for January).
3. Dec 21 to 31: run a "30 day challenge starts Jan 1" countdown. Drive saves ("save this plan") since saves signal intent to return.
4. Jan 1 to 31: daily day-N series (Day 1, Day 2 ...) as a visible streak on the account itself, posted by the builder as "day N of the challenge, here is the tip." This borrows the day-counter trope without a personal recovery claim. **[infer]**
5. Plan for the drop: around Jan 8 to 15 a lot of attempts fail (49% of young participants). Restart-kindly content peaks demand then. Schedule at least 6 restart posts in this window.
6. A Dec 15 content freeze check: Studio schedules 10 days ahead, so queue the Dec 21 to 31 set on Dec 11 and the Jan 1 to 10 set on Dec 22.

### Weekly review routine

1. At 72h per post, log views, saves, shares, comments, profile visits, and "app" comments in `Tracker.csv`.
2. Compute each type's median views and save rate. Rank on views first, saves second.
