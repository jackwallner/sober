## What happens when you log a slip?

| What | After a slip |
| --- | --- |
| Day counter | Restarts. A slip today starts day 1. For a slip on a past date, the days since then are counted. |
| Bonsai | Keeps half of its growth |
| Past streaks | Stay on your record |
| Longest streak | Kept, worked out from the corrected dates |
| Calendar | Every sober day you counted stays, and the slip day is marked |

Before you confirm, the slip screen shows exactly what you keep, including how many days of growth the tree carries over.

## Why does the counter still restart?

So the number stays honest. The counter shows your current run of alcohol-free days. The garden, the calendar and your past streaks hold the rest of the story.

## How do you log a slip?

1. On Home, tap **I slipped**. Or open a day in the calendar and tap **Log a slip**, which works for past dates too.
2. Add a mood or a note if you want.
3. Tap **Log it and keep going**.

## Logged a slip by mistake?

Open that day in the calendar and undo it. The counter and the tree go back to how they were. Undo works while it is the most recent slip on your record.

## How do other apps handle a slip?

It varies. Some counters reset everything to zero, and some let you choose to keep the streak running. Sober restarts the count and keeps the garden growth, streak history and calendar. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does Sober reset to zero after a relapse?

The day counter restarts. Your bonsai keeps half its growth, and your past streaks, longest streak and calendar stay.

### Can I log a slip on a past date?

Yes. Open the day in the calendar and log it there. The days since that slip are counted.

### Can I undo a slip?

Yes, from the calendar, while it is the most recent slip on your record. The counter and tree go back to how they were.

### Is logging a slip a Bloom+ feature?

No. Slips, the counter, the calendar and the garden are all free.
