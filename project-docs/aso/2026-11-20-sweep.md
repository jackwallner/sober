Checks after the fixes: a Python scan for placeholders, limits, em dashes, emoji, brand names, keyword rules and trailing fragments (clean); `asc migrate validate --fastlane-dir fastlane` (valid, 0 errors, 0 warnings); `scripts/validate-aso-metadata.py` and `scripts/validate-asc-metadata.py` (exit 0).

## Could not judge without a native speaker

- bn-BD name ends `ট্র্যাক` (cut from ট্র্যাকার by the limit); reads as a noun-less "track". ml-IN, ta-IN, or-IN first description sentence ends `... সাহায্য করে` / `உதவுகிறது` / `സഹായിക്കുന്നു` with no stated goal (help you to stay alcohol-free). Both are readable, left alone.
- Indic keywords for recovery and relapse (bn পুনরুদ্ধার/পুনরাবৃত্তি, gu, mr, te, hi सुधार/पुनरावृत्ति) use words that lean to restoration or repetition; kept because no clear wrong meaning was provable. pa-IN and ta-IN had clear ones and were fixed.
- "Dry days" is a literal calque in ar, th (วันแห้ง), ml (ഉണങ്ങിയ ദിവസം), ta (உலர் நாள்), bn, hi, vi (Ngày khô). Understandable, probably unnatural. It matches `días secos`/`jours secs` in locales where autocomplete showed it ranks, but no autocomplete evidence exists for the Asian and Indic ones.
- id and ms name `Berhenti alkohol` omits `minum` (telegraphic, understandable). it/pt/ro/es-ES subtitles `Contatore sobrietà & giardino`, `Contador recuperação & jardim`, `Contor recuperare & grădină` omit the linking `di`/`de` (no room in 30 characters).
- cs, sk, hr, id, el, tr, he use informal address in the promo and formal address in the description. Each text is internally consistent, so it was left.
- zh-Hans/zh-Hant keywords keep `戒断/戒斷` and fr-FR keeps `sevrage` (withdrawal). They are search terms, not claims, but fr-CA dropped `sevrage` on App Review 1.4.1 grounds.

## Decisions for Jack

1. Most older descriptions are the short 2026 first draft (about 550 bytes). They have no subscription renewal and cancellation paragraph, no "not medical care" disclaimer, and no mention of the goal countdown, craving mode, slips or three widgets that en-US has. Only ar-SA carries the disclaimer. Recommend rebuilding them from the current en-US description the way the ten rewritten locales were; that is a rewrite, so it was not done here.
2. Subtitles of the form `X & garden` (most locales) are not wrong, the app does have a virtual garden, but read as filler next to en-US `Quit Drinking, Grow Each Day`. Only the cut-off sl-SI one was fixed. Recommend replacing them in the rewrite in item 1.
3. Names no longer carry `& app` / `app` filler in 18 locales; several are now well under 30 characters, so there is room for a better phrase if you want keyword research later.
