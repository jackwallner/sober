# Brief 05 — Onboarding illustrations

Three illustrations for the three-screen onboarding flow.

## Flow (planned)

1. **Welcome** — "Track every sober day."
2. **Garden grows with you** — "Your bonsai matures alongside your streak."
3. **Set your start date** — "Pick the day your journey began."

## Per-illustration spec

- 600×600 SVG, transparent background.
- Each illustration should focus on **one object** with breathing room around it.
- Style matches brief 01 (flat painterly, palette colors, slight asymmetry).

## Concepts

| Screen          | Subject                                           |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Welcome         | A single pot with a fresh sprout. Hopeful.        |
| Garden grows    | Three small bonsai side-by-side, day 7 → 60 → 180. Progression visible. |
| Set start date  | A calendar page with a single dot, lifted gently. Or a hand placing the seed in the pot. |

## Output

```
output/onboarding/

└── onboard-03-start-date.svg
```
