Sober Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Sober is a personal sobriety tracking app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It does not require an account and does not read or write Apple Health.

Data stored on your devices

Sobriety journey history, check-ins, mood, notes, journal entries, garden state, achievements, savings estimates, settings, and reminder preferences are stored locally in SwiftData, UserDefaults, and an App Group shared with the Watch and widgets. This information is not uploaded.

Purchases

Bloom+ is offered through monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes payment. RevenueCat verifies purchase status and may process an anonymous app user ID, purchase and entitlement information, and limited technical context. It does not receive your journal, check-ins, or other app content. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

No account, server-side usage analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking, location, contacts, photos, or HealthKit data. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it in the app, where available, or remove the app. Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction records as needed to process and restore purchases. Email support messages contain whatever you choose to send and are used to answer your request.

Children and changes

Sober is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the app or its services change.
