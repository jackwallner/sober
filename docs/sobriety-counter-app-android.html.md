## Is there a sobriety counter app for Android?

By Jack Wallner · Last updated October 30, 2026

**Yes. [Sober: Sobriety Day Counter](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jackwallner.sober) is a sobriety counter app for Android, and it is on Google Play.** It counts your alcohol-free days, has a daily check-in, a calendar, a guided breathing session for cravings and a Home Screen widget, and it needs no account.

**Disclosure:** Jack Wallner makes Sober. What the Android app does below was checked against its code and its Google Play listing as of October 2026.

General information, not medical advice. Sober is a wellness tracker, not treatment. If stopping alcohol could put you at risk of withdrawal, talk to a clinician first. In the US, the SAMHSA National Helpline offers free, confidential help around the clock at 1-800-662-4357.

## What does Sober do on Android?

- **Day counter:** your current streak, your longest streak and every alcohol-free day, counted from the start date you set.
- **Daily check-in:** one tap, with an optional mood and a short note.
- **Calendar:** a timeline that shows how your bonsai looked on any day.
- **Bonsai garden:** a tree that grows with each alcohol-free day, and a new tree joins your grove each full year.
- **Craving mode:** a guided box-breathing session for an urge to drink, free and unlimited.
- **Slips:** you log a slip honestly, the count starts over, your history stays and the tree keeps half its growth.
- **Money and calories:** the totals you have kept so far, from the daily numbers you enter.
- **Health milestones:** from the first day up to a year, with sources.
- **Reminders:** optional, and you can turn them off.

## What does the Android widget show?

The Home Screen widget shows your day count with your bonsai. Stretch it wider and it also shows your tree stage and the date you started. Tapping it opens the app. To add it, touch and hold an empty spot on your Home screen, open **Widgets** and choose **Sober Tracker**.

## What is free and what needs Bloom+?

The features in the list above are free and do not expire. Of the health milestones, the first five are free. Bloom+ is an optional upgrade that adds:

- Your craving patterns: when cravings hit, what sets them off and how long yours usually last.
- The full 13-milestone health timeline with sources.
- Your year ahead: the money and calories you would keep by this time next year.
- A daily journal with prompts.
- All 6 bonsai species.

Bloom+ is a monthly or yearly subscription, with a free trial for eligible new subscribers, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Payment goes through your Google Play account, and prices are shown in the app before you buy.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Sober available on Android?

Yes. Sober: Sobriety Day Counter is on Google Play for Android 8.0 and newer. After you install it, the app shows on your phone as Sober Tracker.

### Is the Sober Android app free?

Yes. It is free to install, and the day counter, daily check-in, calendar, craving mode and Home Screen widget are free. Bloom+ is an optional upgrade.

### Does Sober have an Android widget?

Yes. A Home Screen widget shows your day count with your bonsai, and a wider size adds your tree stage and start date. It can be resized.

### Does Sober for Android need an account?

No. You can use the whole free app without making an account, and your check-ins, journal and cravings stay on your phone.

### Is the Android app the same as the iPhone app?

It is a port of the iPhone app with the same counter, check-in, calendar, craving mode, bonsai and Bloom+ split. The Apple Watch app and the Lock Screen widgets are iPhone only.
