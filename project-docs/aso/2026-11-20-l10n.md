# Localized metadata from keyword research, 2026-11-20

Ten locales rewritten: en-GB, en-CA, fr-CA, en-AU, uk, pl, de-DE, ru, nl-NL, es-MX. Nothing was pushed to App Store Connect. Descriptions were rebuilt from the current en-US description (free trial length is shown in the app, no fixed "7 days"; watch has no complication; three Lock Screen and two Home Screen widgets). Check script: `scripts/check-aso-locales.py`. Repo validator: `scripts/validate-aso-metadata.py`.

## Method and limits of the evidence
- Indexing from Apple's localizations table (fetched 2026-11-20). Storefront default plus additional languages:
  - United Kingdom, Ireland, South Africa, Philippines, Estonia and about 100 more: en-GB only.
  - Ukraine: en-GB, with Russian and Ukrainian added. Poland: en-GB, with Polish added.
  - Germany, Austria, Switzerland: German, with en-GB added (Switzerland also French and Italian). Netherlands: Dutch, with en-GB added. Belgium: en-GB, with Dutch and French added.
  - Mexico: es-MX, with en-GB added. Russia: ru, with en-GB and Ukrainian added. Kazakhstan: en-GB only.
  - Canada: en-CA and fr-CA only (no en-GB, no en-US). Australia and New Zealand: en-AU, with en-GB added.
  - United States: en-US, with es-MX, ru, zh, fr, ko, pt-BR, vi, ar-SA added.
- Consequence: en-GB is the primary or secondary English list for Ukraine and Poland (our 2nd and 5th biggest download markets) and for Germany, Mexico, Netherlands, Russia and Australia. The Ukrainian and Polish top results for almost every query are English-titled apps, so English terms in en-GB carry real weight there.
- Autocomplete: Apple search hints per storefront (GB, ZA, IE, AU, NZ, CA, UA, PL, DE, AT, CH, RU, KZ, NL, BE, MX, FR). Ukrainian and Polish hints are thin (many seeds return nothing); Dutch, German, Russian and Spanish are rich.
- Ranks: iTunes Search API, 200 results, our trackId 6768869215, taken 2026-11-20. Caveat: the API returns no record for our app in PL, DE and NL (lookup returns 0) and returned far fewer results than the 2026-10-28 pass measured, so the rank snapshots for those three are carried from `2026-10-28-aso-pass.md` and are not new measurements. An autocomplete suggestion is not a traffic measurement.
- Competitor subtitles read from apps.apple.com. Competitor brand names appear below only as evidence and in no field.
