No account, login, or personal information is required. All data (sobriety dates, check-ins, journal, garden) is stored on-device in the app's App Group container. The app does not use non-exempt encryption.

GETTING STARTED
- On first launch, onboarding sets the sobriety start date and optional daily spending so money/calories saved can be estimated. You can pick any start date (including today or a past date).
- Today tab: day counter, daily check-in, and the garden that grows as sober days accrue.
- Timeline tab: every sober day. Garden collection: the bonsai species.

TESTING BLOOM+ (the in-app subscription) AT NO CHARGE
Bloom+ unlocks the full health-benefit timeline, journal compose, achievements, money/calories-saved stats, additional garden species, and Watch/widget extras. It is offered as an auto-renewable subscription (monthly / yearly, both with a free trial) and a one-time lifetime purchase.

To review Bloom+ features without being charged:
1. Open any locked feature - e.g. tap a blurred milestone in the Health tab, "Compose" in Journal, or the Bloom+ row in Settings - to bring up the paywall.
2. Complete the purchase using the App Review sandbox Apple ID. Sandbox purchases are not charged.
3. All Bloom+ features unlock immediately. "Restore Purchases" on the paywall also works with the sandbox account.

If a sandbox purchase cannot be completed during review, please contact us at jackwallner@gmail.com and we will provide an alternate way to evaluate the Pro features promptly.
