## Is there a sober counter with an Apple Watch app and a lock screen widget?

By Jack Wallner · Last updated October 30, 2026

**Yes. Sober: Sobriety Day Counter has an Apple Watch app and three Lock Screen widgets that show your days sober, and all of them are free.** [Get Sober on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sober-sobriety-day-counter/id6768869215?pt=128748779&ct=jw-sober-sober-counter-apple-watch-lock-screen-widget&mt=8). It also has small and medium Home Screen widgets with your bonsai, and it needs no account.

General information, not medical advice. Sober is a wellness tracker, not treatment. If stopping alcohol could put you at risk of withdrawal, talk to a clinician first. In the US, the SAMHSA National Helpline offers free, confidential help around the clock at 1-800-662-4357.

## Which widgets does Sober have?

| Widget | Where | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Circular | Lock Screen | Your day count |
| Rectangular | Lock Screen | A leaf and "days sober" with your count |
| Inline | Lock Screen, above the clock | Your count as a line of text |
| Small | Home Screen | Your bonsai with the day count |
| Medium | Home Screen | Your bonsai, day count, tree stage and start date |

The widgets count from your start date, so they roll over at midnight even if you have not opened the app.

## What does the Apple Watch app show?

Open Sober on your Watch to see your day count, the date you started and your bonsai's current stage. The Watch works the count out from your start date too, so it moves forward each day on its own.

The Watch app is for seeing your count. Check-ins, slips and the craving breathing session happen on your iPhone, and the Watch picks up the change.

## How do you add the Lock Screen widget?

1. Install Sober and set your start date.
2. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, then tap **Customize** and choose the Lock Screen.
3. Tap the widget area under the clock (or the line above it for the inline widget) and pick **Sober**.
4. On Apple Watch, Sober installs with the iPhone app when automatic install is on, or from **Available Apps** in the Watch app on your iPhone.

## What Sober's Watch app does not do yet

- There is no watch face complication. For a number you can see all the time, use the Lock Screen widget on your iPhone.
- You cannot check in from the Watch.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Sober's widgets free?

Yes. All five widgets, three for the Lock Screen and two for the Home Screen, are in the free version.

### Is the Apple Watch app free?

Yes. It comes with the free iPhone app and shows your day count, start date and tree stage.

### Can I check in from my Apple Watch?

Not yet. The Watch app shows your count. Check-ins happen on your iPhone.

### Does Sober have a watch face complication?

Not yet. The Lock Screen widgets on iPhone are the always-visible option today.

### Do I need an account for the Watch to sync?

No. Your iPhone shares the count with your own Watch directly, with no account.
