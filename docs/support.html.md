## Sober Support

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Sober is a sobriety tracking app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It helps you track your journey with a day counter, virtual garden, health benefit timeline, journaling, achievements, and more. All data stays on your device, with no account required.

## Getting Started

- **Onboarding:** When you first open Sober, you'll set your start date, daily spending estimate, daily calorie estimate, and reminder time. You can change any of these later in Settings.
- **Daily check-in:** Open the Today tab and tap the check-in button once per day. This waters your garden and logs the day as sober.
- **Resetting:** If you need to reset your sobriety counter, tap the reset button in the Today view toolbar. Your previous journey history is preserved.

## Features

- **Today:** Day counter, garden, check-in button, next milestone, next health benefit.
- **Calendar:** Color-coded monthly view: green (sober), red (not sober), gray (not checked in).
- **Health:** 13 health benefits on a recovery timeline from NIAAA, CDC, and AHA. The first 5 are free; Bloom+ adds the other 8.
- **Journal:** Daily reflection prompts with a feeling picker. Requires Pro.
- **Achievements:** 9 milestones across time and check-in streaks. Requires Pro.
- **Stats:** Money and calories saved since starting. Requires Pro.
- **Garden:** Virtual garden with 6 bonsai styles plus decorations you earn over time. The Traditional bonsai is free; Cascading, Cherry Blossom, Japanese Maple, Black Pine, and Windswept come with Bloom+.

## Troubleshooting

- **Garden not growing:** Make sure you check in each day. The garden progresses based on your current streak length.
- **Widgets not updating:** Open Sober once to refresh the widget cache. Widgets update every 30 minutes.
- **Check-in not working:** You can only check in once per day for the current day. Check your Today view to see today's status.
- **Data not appearing:** All data is stored locally via SwiftData. If you're seeing corruption, the app will automatically attempt recovery.

## Bloom+ Subscriptions

- **Upgrade:** Open Settings → Bloom+ → Upgrade, or tap any locked Pro feature in the app.
- **Free trial:** New subscribers may be eligible for a 7-day free trial on monthly or yearly plans (offered by Apple; eligibility varies).
- **Restore:** Settings → Bloom+ → Restore Purchases, or Restore Purchases on the upgrade screen.
- **Manage or cancel:** iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Sober. Lifetime purchases do not renew.
- **Refunds:** Request through Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Privacy

Sober does not create an account, does not send your data to any server, and does not include analytics, ads, or tracking. All data is stored locally on your device.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://jackwallner.com/ios/sober/privacy-policy.html).
