## Sobriety Calculator: Money and Calories Saved Not Drinking

By Jack Wallner · Last updated November 18, 2026

Enter how many drinks you had in a week and what each one cost. The calculator shows the money and calories that stay with you over a week, a month, a year and five years. Add your sober date to see your day count and the totals so far.

General information, not medical advice. Sober is a tracking tool and does not diagnose or treat anything. If you drink heavily, talk to a doctor before you stop, because sudden withdrawal can be dangerous.

## How is the money saved worked out?

Drinks per week times price per drink gives a weekly figure. Divide by 7 for a day, then multiply by 365 for a year. At 14 drinks a week and $8 a drink, that is $112 a week, $16 a day and $5,840 a year. Use the price you really paid: a bar pour with a tip costs several times what the same drink costs at home.

## What do the calories add up to?

The line under the big number compares the yearly calories with body fat, using the common figure of 3,500 calories per pound (7,700 per kilogram). It is an energy comparison. What happens to your weight depends on what you eat and drink instead, how much you move, and your own body.

## How are days sober counted?

From your sober date through today, with the sober date as day 1. Someone who stopped on January 1 is on day 31 on January 31. The totals so far are that day count times your daily money and calories.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much money do you save by not drinking?

Multiply your drinks per week by what each one cost. At 14 drinks a week and $8 a drink that is $112 a week, $16 a day and $5,840 a year.

### How many calories are in an alcoholic drink?

MedlinePlus lists about 153 calories in a 12 oz regular beer, 103 in a light beer, 125 in a 5 oz glass of red wine, 121 in white wine, 97 in a 1.5 oz shot of 80-proof spirits and 168 in a 4 oz margarita.

### How do I work out how many days sober I am?

Count the days from your sober date through today, with the sober date as day 1. Someone who stopped on January 1 is on day 31 on January 31.

### Will I lose weight if I stop drinking?

It depends on what replaces the drinks. The calculator shows the calories that were in the drinks themselves. Your weight also depends on food, activity and your body, so the body fat figure is an energy comparison, not a forecast.

### Does this calculator save what I type?

No. The math runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere or stored, and it is gone when you close the page.
