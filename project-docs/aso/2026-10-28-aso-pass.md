## Review decisions

**Verdict: incomplete.** The independently checked queries exposed errors, but do not cover every realistic removed-token combination or ten claims per locale. Native-copy quality and the US secondary-field plan need further review before this pass can be approved.
- English descriptions and promotional text retain the tier update. Garden copy now says that some growth survives a slip, consistent with `GardenService.recordSlip`.
- en-CA, en-GB and en-AU retain their original keyword fields. Live results show the removed `diary` supports stronger ranks than `dry days`.
- Ukrainian and Polish names, subtitles and keywords retain the baseline. The proposed titles target unranked or #24 phrases, while the original word pools support observed top-10 searches; Ukrainian `сухі дні` and `кинь алкоголь` both rank #1. Polish `rzuć alkohol` ranks #5, `suche dni` #1, and the removed keyword supports `nastrój alkohol` at #1. The earlier report incorrectly said both Polish title phrases were unranked. These results do not justify the title risk.
- German keywords retain the baseline: `stimmung alkohol` ranks #3. Dutch and Russian keyword fields retain the baseline because `nuchter stemming` ranks #4 and `настроение алкоголь` ranks #1.
- Apple lists US supported display languages in its [localizations reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations). That table does not itself prove cross-locale search indexing.
- Mexican keywords retain the baseline because `dejar de beber` ranks #18. Arabic keywords retain the baseline because the removed terms lack sufficient same-locale checks. The proposed US secondary-locale optimization is not established.
- Russian copy fixes the renewal rule: cancellation must occur at least 24 hours before the period ends.
- Six previously existing em dashes were removed from Hebrew, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and both Chinese descriptions. Korean `앱` was removed from keywords because it repeats the subtitle.
- The existing `dryday` token is repeated across zh-Hans, zh-Hant and ko. Distinct, complete English query groups for the US secondary fields remain unverified and are not established by this review.
- The earlier report overstated the certainty of several decisions. Its ranks and autocomplete findings were not independently verified in full; an autocomplete suggestion is not a traffic measurement. Before release, native-language copy review and a complete combination-by-combination SERP audit remain necessary.
