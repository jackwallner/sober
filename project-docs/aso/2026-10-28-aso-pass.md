# App Store ASO pass, 2026-10-28

Snapshot: 2026-10-28 Pacific time, before any upload. Rank is the app's position in Apple's software search results, with the supplied app ID checked in the first 200 results. `NR` means the app was absent from the returned results. Autocomplete came from Apple's MZSearchHints endpoint. Search volume/popularity data was not used. The last-90-day customer counts supplied for prioritization were US 226, Canada 89, UK 83, Ukraine 75, Poland 59, Germany 59, Netherlands 30, Russia 20, and Australia 18.

## Decisions

- Keep the en-US title, subtitle, and keyword field. The latest en-US keyword restoration is commit `bb3bd87` from 2026-10-28. It restores `cravings`, `checkin`, and `private` after the 2026-09-30 keyword swap. Live ranks remain #4 for `sobriety calendar`, #5 for `sober day counter`, and #8 for `sobriety day counter`. The app-level demotion reported on 2026-10-08 affected every phrase on the same day, so this pass makes no US title churn.
- The June 25 ASO plan recommended holding the US title and subtitle, with `Dry Days: Sobriety Counter` as its suggested subtitle. The current App Store fields are `Sober: Sobriety Day Counter` and `Quit Drinking, Grow Each Day`; current rank evidence supports keeping them.
- Update descriptions and promos to the current product boundaries. The current guide makes the first five health benefits, earned badges, money and calories saved so far, Apple Watch, and widgets free. Bloom+ has personal craving patterns and trigger plans, projections, the full 13-milestone health timeline with sources, journal prompts, and additional garden species. The previous English copy described several free features as paid and included cutting back language that does not match the abstinence positioning in the current plan.
- Title and subtitle changes are limited to Ukrainian and Polish. Both edits keep the same locale words that form the observed top-ten queries. Dutch and Russian titles and subtitles stay unchanged because their current phrases still rank first or within the top ten.
- Apple lists es-MX, ar, ru, zh-Hans, zh-Hant, fr-FR, pt-BR, vi, and ko as US storefront indexed localizations. en-US stays the primary English field. This pass uses es-MX for the winnable `sober garden` phrase and ar-SA for `craving tracker`, while keeping the existing Spanish and Arabic keyword tokens that serve those languages. Apple localization reference: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations

## Risks and verification

- The search snapshot is pre-release. Rank changes from the new metadata can only be measured after indexing; no metadata was uploaded in this run.
- Ukrainian and Polish title changes are the largest ranking risks. Their observed top-ten phrases retain the needed words within the same locale, but Apple can reorder results after a title change.
- The App Store API returned 429 and 403 errors during the initial NL and RU sweep. Calls resumed after the cooldown, and the ranks above come from successful retries. The final NL and RU snapshots use current results rather than treating rate-limit failures as NR.
- The copy changes align descriptions with the current product guide. Localization remains a language-quality risk, especially the Arabic and Russian subscription/legal phrasing.
- `scripts/validate-aso-metadata.py` checks all five App Store fields for length limits, edge whitespace, em dash, duplicate keyword tokens, and title/subtitle token overlap. It passed for all 11 touched locales: en-US, en-CA, en-GB, en-AU, uk, pl, de-DE, nl-NL, ru, es-MX, and ar-SA.
