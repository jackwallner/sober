# Hooks v2: the mirror (2026-11-04)

Target: someone scrolling at random who drinks more than they admit. The first line names something they thought was private. The second opens a door. Each runs as a 5 to 9 second video over real footage, with the detail in the caption.

## Rules

- Name the behavior, in the viewer's own private details. A specific secret beats a general warning.
- It should sting because it's accurate. Skip insults and labels: no "alcoholic", no "you have a problem", no quiz results.
- The last beat always opens a door, because discomfort with no way out just sends people away.
- No invented stories or testimonials, no health claims without a source, no alcohol on screen.
- Captions on the heaviest posts carry: "If you drink heavily every day, talk to a doctor before you stop. Withdrawal can be dangerous. US: SAMHSA's free helpline is 1-800-662-4357, 24/7."

## Secrets

1. You told your doctor half the real number. / What's the real number?
2. You've googled "am I drinking too much" more than once. / You already know why you keep asking.
3. You bought it at a different store this time. / You know exactly why.
4. You take the recycling out when nobody's home. / Who are you hiding it from?
5. You keep a count in your head so nobody else can. / That's a lot of work for something you're fine with.
6. You pour a little more, then top the bottle up to where it was. / You're tired. You can stop doing this.

## The people watching

7. Somebody in your house counts your drinks too. / They just stopped saying it out loud.
8. Your kids know what your voice sounds like after the second glass. / They'd love to hear the other one at bedtime.
9. Your partner goes to bed early now. / Have you asked why?
10. Your friends plan around how you get after three. / You could give them a different night.
11. Your mom asked if you're okay. / She's been practicing that question for months.

## The morning after

12. You read your texts to find out what kind of night it was.
13. 3 AM. Heart pounding. Replaying everything you said. / You could sleep through tonight.
14. You remember the fight. You can't remember what you said. / They can.
15. You apologized this morning without knowing what for.
16. You're being extra nice today. Everyone in your house knows why.

## The bargaining

17. You're waiting for it to get bad enough. / You're allowed to stop before that.
18. "I'll take this week off." How many Mondays is that now? / Today could be the one that sticks.
19. You made a rule. Only weekends. Then Thursday became a weekend.
20. You've stopped telling yourself "just one." Now you just skip that part.
21. You're fine. You've worked really hard on being fine. / What would it feel like to stop working so hard?
22. Everyone thinks you've got it under control. You're very good at that.
23. You've already decided you're drinking tonight. It's 2 PM.

## What it's taking

24. You can't remember the last Sunday you actually had.
25. You were there. You just can't remember the end of it.
26. When did every plan start needing a drink in it?
27. Picture one year from today, exactly the same. / How does that feel?

## Topical, this month

28. Thanksgiving's in three weeks. You're already planning how much you can get away with.
29. Sober October's over. You counted down to it, didn't you?
30. Everyone's going to see you at the holiday party. You're already nervous about which version.
