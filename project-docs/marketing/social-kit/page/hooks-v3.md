# Hooks v3: the mirror, standing alone (2026-11-04)

Replaces `hooks-v2.md` after Jack's review: a scroller has no context, so the first line itself has to say this is about drinking. No app anywhere in this phase: no slides, no captions, disclosure off. `posts_v3.py` holds what actually renders.

## Rules

- The first line carries a drinking cue in the viewer's own words: drinks, wine, bottle, empties, hungover, drunk. Plain enough to read in one second, and no clinical words.
- Name the behavior in private detail. A specific secret beats a general warning.
- It should sting because it's accurate. No labels: no "alcoholic", no "you have a problem", no quiz results.
- The last beat opens a door, because discomfort with no way out just sends people away.
- No invented stories or testimonials, no health claims without a source. No alcohol being poured or drunk on screen, no minors, and no identifiable person in footage: silhouettes, rooms, glass, rain.
- Captions on heavy posts carry: "If you drink heavily every day, talk to a doctor before you stop. Withdrawal can be dangerous. US: SAMHSA's free helpline is 1-800-662-4357, 24/7."

## Secrets

1. You told your doctor 2 drinks a night. / What's the real number?
2. You've googled "am I drinking too much" more than once. / You already know why you keep asking.
3. You bought the wine at a different store this time. / You know exactly why.
4. You take the empties out when nobody's home. / Who are you hiding them from?
5. You keep a count of your drinks so nobody else can. / That's a lot of work for something you're fine with.
6. You topped the vodka up with water so nobody would notice. / You're tired. You can stop doing this.

## The people watching

7. Somebody in your house counts your drinks too. / They just stopped saying it out loud.
8. Your kids know what your voice sounds like after the second glass. / They'd love to hear the other one at bedtime.
9. Your partner goes to bed before you open the second bottle now. / Have you asked why?
10. Your friends plan around how you get after three drinks. / You could give them a different night.
11. Your mom asked how much you've been drinking lately. / She practiced that question for months.

## The morning after

12. You read your texts to find out how drunk you got. / You could wake up already knowing.
13. 3 AM. Heart pounding. Last night's wine wearing off. / You could sleep through tonight.
14. You remember the fight. Six drinks in, you can't remember what you said. / They can.
15. You apologized this morning for something you said drunk. / You could have a morning with nothing to fix.
16. You're extra nice the morning after drinking. Everyone in your house knows why. / You could have a morning with nothing to make up for.

## The bargaining

17. You're waiting for your drinking to get bad enough. / You're allowed to stop before that.
18. "I'll take this week off drinking." How many Mondays is that now? / Today could be the one that sticks.
19. You made a rule. Only drink on weekends. Then Thursday became a weekend. / You know where this goes. You can get off here.
20. You've stopped saying "just one drink." Now you skip that part. / You still get to decide about tonight.
21. You've gotten really good at seeming sober. / What would it feel like to stop working so hard?
22. Everyone thinks your drinking's under control. You're very good at that. / You can put that down.
23. You've already decided you're drinking tonight. It's 2 PM. / You've got hours to change your mind.

## What it's taking

24. You can't remember the last Sunday you didn't wake up hungover. / That one can be next Sunday.
25. You were there. The drinks erased the end of it. / You could keep the whole night.
26. When did every plan start needing a drink in it? / Try one plan without it this week.
27. Picture one more year of drinking exactly like this. / How does that feel?
