# Screenshots — Claude Design brief

> **Goal:** 5 App Store screenshots (1290×2796, iPhone 6.7" Pro Max) for Sober.
> **Only output:** Five PNG files. No icons, no illustrations, no art assets — just the screenshots.

## What to show

Each screenshot is a real iPhone screen from the Sober app, with the app's actual UI. No marketing chrome, no background art — the template already handles that. Claude Design only provides the 5 screenshots themselves.

### 1. Home — "Your sober days, growing."

Show the main screen with a day counter at ~Day 47. The UI should show:
- Large day count: "47 dry days" (or similar)
- Today's check-in mood (a small emoji or state indicator)
- A streak indicator (longest streak, current streak)
- The next milestone callout (e.g. "Day 50: Liver recovery milestone")
- Minimal, clean, calm design — earth tones, no bright colors
- No account/onboarding chrome — this is the in-app experience

### 2. Garden — "A bonsai that grows with you."

Show the garden scene with a bonsai tree at ~Day 47 maturity. The UI should show:
- A bonsai tree center-stage that looks partway grown (not a seedling, not fully mature)
- Some indication of interactive elements (tappable decorations or items around the tree)
- The day counter in the corner showing it's tied to sobriety
- Calm, green, natural color palette
- The tree should look healthy and cared for — this is aspirational

### 3. Health — "See how your body recovers."

Show the health benefits timeline. The UI should show:
- A timeline or list of health milestones with days (Day 1: Blood pressure drops, Day 2: Hangxiety gone, Day 7: Sleep improves, Day 30: Liver recovery, Day 60: Heart health)
- The current day highlighted (Day 47) showing what's unlocking
- Progress indicators toward the next milestone
- Clean medical-style typography, not cartoonish

### 4. Journal — "Reflect each day."

Show the daily journal compose screen. The UI should show:
- A writing card with a prompt like "How are you feeling today?"
- A mood selector (small icons for different moods)
- A sample reflection written: "Day 47. Energy is way up. Slept 7.5 hours for the third night in a row."
- Minimal, focused, reading-first layout

### 5. Bloom+ — "Bloom+ when you're ready."

Show the premium upgrade screen. The UI should show:
- What Pro unlocks: health timeline, journal with prompts, all 3 bonsai species, garden decorations, Apple Watch app, home screen widgets
- A pricing callout: "Free trial. Cancel anytime."
- The garden/plant theme continuing visually
- Clean, conversion-focused layout (not the current paywall — redesign it for the screenshot)

## What NOT to do

- Do NOT create any icons, app icon, or standalone illustrations
- Do NOT output marketing background images or wallpapers
- Do NOT create anything outside the 5 screenshots
- Do NOT design screens that don't exist in the app — these must be achievable in the real app
- Do NOT use bright party colors — the app is calm, warm, earthy

## Constraints

- Output format: 1290×2796 PNG at 3× (or SVG that fits exactly)
- Dark backgrounds are okay for the screenshots (the app can use dark mode)
- Everything should read clearly at thumbnail size in App Store search results
- The screenshots go inside a phone mockup with marketing copy above them — keep the top ~25% of the screenshot clear for the phone's status bar area (the template overlays the Dynamic Island)

## Delivery

Put files in `claude-design-handoff/output/screenshots/` as:
- `screenshot-01-home.png`
- `screenshot-02-garden.png`
- `screenshot-03-health.png`
- `screenshot-04-journal.png`
- `screenshot-05-bloom+.png`

No variants. No alternate directions. One pass, five files, done.
