Use Sober for a dry month, for cutting back, or to count sober time with no end date, one day at a time.

Eligible new subscribers can try Bloom+ free for 7 days. Bloom+ is available monthly or yearly, plus a one-time lifetime option. Prices appear in the app before you buy and vary by region.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID after trial confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account is charged within 24 hours before renewal. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Sober is a wellness tracker, not medical care, diagnosis, or treatment. If stopping alcohol may put you at risk of withdrawal, contact a qualified clinician.
