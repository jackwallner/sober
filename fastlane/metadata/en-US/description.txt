Sober is a free app that counts the days since you stopped drinking alcohol. It shows your sober day count on your iPhone, your Lock Screen, and your Apple Watch, with no account to create.

Use it for Dry January, a sober month, cutting back, or long-term sobriety.

DAY COUNTER (FREE)
• Counts alcohol-free days from the start date you set
• Shows your longest streak and your next milestone
• Counts down to a goal: 30 days, 90 days, a year, or a date you pick
• Sends an optional daily reminder at the hour you choose

CHECK-IN AND CALENDAR (FREE)
• Logs one daily check-in with your mood and a note
• Marks each day on a calendar as sober or a slip
• Grows a bonsai with every sober day

SLIPS (FREE)
• Restarts the counter from the date of a slip you log
• Keeps half your bonsai's growth, your calendar, and your past streaks after a slip

CRAVINGS (FREE)
• Starts guided box breathing in one tap when a craving hits
• Paces each breath in four 4-second phases for about three minutes

WIDGETS AND APPLE WATCH (FREE)
• Shows your day count in three Lock Screen and two Home Screen widgets
• Shows your day count, start date, and tree stage in the Apple Watch app

MONEY AND HEALTH (FREE)
• Shows money saved and calories avoided since your start date
• Shows the first five health milestones
• Awards badges as you reach milestones

PRIVACY (FREE)
• Works with no account or sign-up
• Stores your data on your iPhone and shows no ads

FREE / BLOOM+
Everything above is free. The optional Bloom+ upgrade:
• Shows your craving patterns: when cravings hit and what sets them off
• Builds a plan for each common trigger, with a line to say when offered a drink
• Projects savings and milestones to your goal date and a year ahead
• Unlocks the full 13-milestone health timeline with sources
• Adds a journal with prompts and more bonsai species

Bloom+ is monthly, yearly, or a lifetime purchase, with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. Prices appear in the app before you buy.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Sober is a wellness tracker, not medical care, diagnosis, or treatment. If stopping alcohol may put you at risk of withdrawal, contact a qualified clinician.
