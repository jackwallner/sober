Part 1 · App icon — four concepts

Subject and composition vary; rendering style is the same across all four (soft 3D, single subject, simple background, no fine detail that disappears at 60px). All four favor the growth/garden metaphor rather than the recovery clichés the brief calls out.

First leaf01
A single new sprout-leaf with a dew highlight. Cleanest silhouette of the four — reads as "fresh growth" even at 60×60. Most ASO-friendly.
Bonsai silhouette02
The maturing bonsai itself — the emotional spine of the product, made the icon. Most "Bloom-y." Risk: more parts to render small.
Vessel03
The pot with a single sprout — vessel-as-self. Warmest of the four; reads as patience and tending rather than performance.
Bloom04
A single blossom opening — plays the name straight. Most chromatic; differentiates hardest from competing wellness apps that lean green.

Part 2 · Visual system — Stage A · three directions

Each direction is a paragraph plus a single comparable sample: the Home spine (counter + label + check-in button) at native iPhone width. Same content, three personalities. None are tints of the same thing — they differ in palette mood, type personality, and density.

Direction 01

Slow morning

Closest to the current sage+sand palette, but slowed down. A warm cream backdrop replaces neutral system gray; a deeper moss anchors the brand; the day counter is set in a humanist serif italic so the number reads as handwritten in the journal rather than punched out of a tracker. Type pairs the serif display with a clean grotesque for UI. Generous spacing, soft single-step shadows, no chromatic energy. The recovery framing: this is the app you open in early morning with a cup of tea.

Direction 02

Quiet stone

Near-monochrome stone palette with one chlorophyll-green accent. All grotesque type, single weight discipline. The counter is set in the same family as the UI, just larger — no display-type flourish — so the page reads as quiet, almost Japanese tea-house minimal. Tighter density, sharper rectangle CTA instead of a capsule, fine 1px rules instead of cards. The recovery framing: the app for someone who's done with celebratory wellness aesthetics and just wants a thing that respects their seriousness. This is the most differentiated direction from the current look.

Direction 03

Living light

Brighter, more chromatic, more lyrical. Surfaces pick up a dawn gradient (cream → peach → sage) instead of flat fills, so the garden's time-of-day feeling carries into the chrome. The counter is set in a humanist serif italic at a much larger size — almost editorial — paired with a humanist sans body. One coral accent for milestones and unlocks. The recovery framing: this is for users who want their progress to feel alive and forward-leaning, not stoic. Most chromatic energy of the three — also the highest risk of feeling more "wellness app" than the brief's calm-not-clinical register, depending on how restrained the chrome stays.
